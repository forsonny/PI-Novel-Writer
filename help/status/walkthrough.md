# /PNW-status — Walkthrough

1. Run `/PNW-status` after loading.
2. Confirm the total and target, then inspect chapter/scene counts and numeric-gap
   warnings.
3. Do not infer summary health, outline agreement, bible coverage or completed
   editing from this dashboard. Check those separately.
4. If outline-status scenes contain placeholder prose, remember that their words
   are counted.
5. Treat the chapter status badge as a rough display only when statuses are mixed.

For autonomous work, `/PNW-auto status` is the authoritative saved-run view.
