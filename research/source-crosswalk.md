# Source decisions, version 1

Basis: *Beyond Fluency*, 11 September 2026, Chapters 4-6, 11, 19-20 and Appendices A-D. These interfaces implement the supplied proposal; they do not claim measured efficacy.

Chapter 5 enumerates 56 distinctions despite referring to 48. Appendix B consolidates them into 48 cards. `extensions/llgf/taxonomy.ts` preserves every Chapter 5 ID and title and maps each to its Appendix B family. An Appendix B E6 finding cannot silently become either character reset or focalization leakage: retain the family and re-annotate the subtype when evidence permits.

Severity is versioned: Chapter 5 uses 0-4; Appendix B uses 0-3 plus L/U. L (licensed) and U (uncertain) are assessment states, not numerical severity. Retain original values; never rescale a historical assessment automatically. A missing assessment is not zero.

Chapter 5 D/P/M/H means direct/proxy/model-assisted/human. Appendix B uses distributional/passage/manuscript/human. Store explicit evidence method and narrative scope instead of translating the letters. The eight cause families also have different framing; keep the source namespace. A suggested cause is a hypothesis, not a fact established by a repeated phrase.

Chapter 19 / Appendix C R0-R10 are the implementation's named revision vocabulary. Chapter 11 maps canonical continuity to R0, causality/agency to R1, information release to R4/R9, focalization to R2, dialogue to R3, imagery to R5/R8, rhythm to R6, and anti-pattern repair to the indicated pass. Chapter 11 originality and final regression remain separate validators; R7 voice fidelity and R10 line integrity do not replace them. No pass runs without an applicable diagnosis.

The original AVS's I/C/T/B/M/N/A/U components, epistemic uncertainty, quiet-scene functions, paired overcorrection and keep-source decisions remain explicit. Numerical thresholds, packet sizes and sample counts are configurable research starting points, not universal literary optima. Prose-origin detection is not a feature.

The supplied DOCX/PDF describe a companion reproducibility archive, but that archive was not supplied. New schemas and code are implementations derived from the written specification, not copies of a validated reference implementation.
