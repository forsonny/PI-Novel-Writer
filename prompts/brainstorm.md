name: brainstorm
description: Open-ended brainstorming on any topic related to your story
arguments:
  - name: topic
    description: The topic or question to brainstorm about
    variable: $@
---

Let's brainstorm about: **$@**

Consider this from multiple angles:

1. **What are the most obvious possibilities?** Get them out first so we can move past them.
2. **What's the unexpected version?** Flip assumptions. What if the opposite were true?
3. **What serves the story's themes?** Which direction reinforces what your story is really about?
4. **What creates the most conflict?** The choice that makes life hardest for your characters is usually the most interesting.
5. **What has the strongest emotional resonance?** Which option will hit the reader hardest?

For each promising direction, let's explore:
- How it connects to existing plot threads
- What new possibilities it opens
- What problems it creates (problems are good — they're future story fuel)
